How to play

Drag a stone up, down, left or right to flick it. It slides until a post, the shore or a line of stones that cannot move stops it. When it runs into a line that can move, it stops, and the stone at the far end of that line slides on in its place. An anchor stone holds when hit; a frost stone freezes the holes it crosses; a painted stone that falls into an open hole sinks and ends the sheet (Undo brings it back). Put every painted stone on a ring of its colour; clear the sheet within par for three stars.

Scout

Scout shows the next flick of a best line from where you are, or how many flicks to undo first. You start with three and earn one on the first clear of sheets 5, 10, 15 and 20 of each lake (up to five at a time). Scouts are never sold. A sheet cleared after using Scout gives at most two stars. "Scout cannot see that far" costs nothing.

Purchases

Mirror Pond and Fishing Holes are free. Granite Bay, Hoarfrost Reach and Lantern Mere are sold one by one, in that order, or together as All Three Lakes. Bought before, or on another device with the same Apple Account? Open Settings → Restore or Restore purchases in the shop.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.

Privacy Policy

Clackmere · last updated November 18, 2026

Published by Luong Nguyen · lamnv260398@gmail.com

Clackmere is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the best stars on each sheet you have cleared and how many Scout hints you hold.
  • Your settings: whether Sound is on or off.
  • Whether you have bought Granite Bay, Hoarfrost Reach, Lantern Mere or All Three Lakes, and the store's transaction numbers for those purchases, so a purchase the store delivers twice is not counted twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Mirror Pond and Fishing Holes (40 sheets) are free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

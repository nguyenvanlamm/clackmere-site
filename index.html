How it plays

  1. Flick a stone up, down, left or right.
  2. It slides until a post, the shore or a backed-up line stops it.
  3. A knock passes on: the stone you hit sends the last stone of its line sliding.
  4. Fill every ring with its colour. Make par for three stars.

Questions

Does it need a connection?

No. Clackmere plays fully offline; the store is only asked when you open the shop or restore a purchase.

Are there ads, or anything that tracks me?

No ads, no analytics, no accounts. Your progress stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

What if I get stuck?

Undo and Restart are free. Scout shows the next flick of a best line, or how many flicks to undo. You start with three Scouts and earn more on sheets 5, 10, 15 and 20 of each lake; they are never sold. Using one caps that sheet at two stars.

I bought a lake on another device. How do I get it here?

Open Settings and tap Restore, or Restore purchases in the shop, signed in to the same Apple Account.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes: Settings, Sound. The game's own synthesised clacks and chimes mix with your music and stay quiet when your iPhone is on silent.
